# TapCheck — Candidate Pilot Partners

*Phase 0 logistics draft. No outreach sent. All 5 are partner types, not named organizations. Awaiting Steven's approval before any contact.*

## 1. Neighborhood Association / Tenant Council
**Why:** Has an existing resident-to-org reporting relationship; a property manager or board member can serve as the natural moderator.

**What to ask:**
- Do residents currently report water issues anywhere, and what happens to those reports?
- Would the association serve as the first moderator and share our link with residents?
- Are there language or accessibility needs we should design for?

---

## 2. Public Elementary or Middle School
**Why:** Schools have lead/water quality obligations and a central facilities contact; parents are a motivated reporting community.

**What to ask:**
- Has the school had water quality notices (lead testing, boil orders) in the last two years?
- Who currently communicates water safety issues to parents?
- Would staff or students submit reports as part of a pilot?

---

## 3. Community Health Clinic (FQHC or Free Clinic)
**Why:** Clinics in under-resourced areas see water-related health complaints and have deep resident trust.

**What to ask:**
- Do patients raise water quality concerns, and is there any existing way to report them?
- Would clinic staff be willing to triage or verify reports for their neighborhood?
- What patient privacy guardrails would need to be in place?

---

## 4. Public Housing Authority
**Why:** Manages large building stock with shared water infrastructure and a legal reporting obligation to residents; maintenance coordinators can be moderators.

**What to ask:**
- What is the current resident water-issue reporting process, and how long does triage take?
- Would a public map create liability concerns, or would transparency help?
- Could a maintenance coordinator take the moderator role?

---

## 5. Local Environmental Justice NGO
**Why:** Already collects water complaints for advocacy; CSV/GeoJSON export gives them data they can use for grants and reports.

**What to ask:**
- Do you currently collect water complaints from residents, and in what format?
- Would CSV/GeoJSON exports be useful for advocacy or grant reporting?
- Are there communities you work with that have had recent water safety incidents?

---

## Recommendation
**Start with the neighborhood association or housing authority** — both slot TapCheck directly into an existing resident→org reporting flow, with a natural moderator already on staff.  
**Choose the NGO** if export data needs advocacy value for the judges or a post-hackathon grant pitch.
